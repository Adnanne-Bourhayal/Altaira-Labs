# SEO and AI Discoverability Notes

The public website should be easy for search engines and AI answer engines to understand without keyword stuffing or generic marketing language.

## Primary Entity

- Brand: Altaira Labs.
- Category: technology consultancy for SMEs.
- Market signal: focused on Belgium and the Benelux, with Spain as a secondary market.
- Main offer: practical digital systems that help local businesses and small SMEs replace manual work with websites, booking flows, dashboards, automations and client management tools.

## Core Topics

- Technology consulting for SMEs.
- Digital systems for small businesses.
- Automation of repetitive work.
- Custom websites and forms.
- Booking systems.
- Internal dashboards.
- CRM / lead management systems.
- Local business workflow automation.
- Lead, client and service management.

## Semantic Structure

- One clear H1 in the hero.
- H2 sections for businesses, services, calculator and contact.
- Literal section IDs: `industries`, `services`, `calculator`, `contact`.
- Services are named clearly: Professional Websites, Booking Systems, Workflow Automation, Management Dashboards and CRM / Lead Management Systems.
- Sectors are named clearly: Clinics, Car Dealers, Restaurants and Specialty by Sector.
- Clinic copy should prioritise dental clinics, aesthetic clinics, physiotherapy and psychology practices.
- Metadata in `app/layout.tsx` matches the visible page promise.

## Copy Rules

Use concrete language:

- lost leads
- repeated admin work
- messy Excel files
- disconnected tools
- client follow-up
- dashboards
- booking systems
- CRM systems
- software for SMEs
- Belgium / Benelux
- local business automation

Avoid:

- "AI-powered solutions for your future"
- "we empower innovation"
- "unlock your potential"
- fake results
- fake testimonials
- "50+ clients"
- "guaranteed 40% growth"
- "AI-powered everything"
- "enterprise-grade platform"
- "official partner"
- long location-stuffed SEO paragraphs
